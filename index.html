About Us

Connecting Aspiring Students with World-Class Universities

Aditya Overseas Education is a trusted international education consultancy and an integral unit of The Career Catalysts Global (TCCG), headquartered in Chennai. We are committed to helping students pursue quality education abroad through expert guidance and deeply personalised support.

Our team assists students at every stage — from identifying the right course and university, to navigating complex application processes, securing scholarships, and obtaining study visas — ensuring a smooth, confident journey toward a successful international future.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Overseas Education Services

From your first query to your first day on campus — we are with you at every milestone.

🎓

Course & University Selection

We analyse your academic profile, interests, and career goals to recommend the best-fit courses and universities across your preferred destinations.

📝

Application Processing

Our team meticulously prepares and submits your university applications, including SOP writing, reference letters, and document verification.

🛂

Visa Guidance & Assistance

Expert support for student visa applications, interview preparation, documentation checklists, and compliance with embassy requirements.

💰

Scholarship Guidance

We identify scholarship opportunities suited to your profile and assist with applications to reduce your financial burden abroad.

🧳

Pre-Departure Support

Comprehensive orientation covering travel arrangements, accommodation options, cultural adaptation, and what to expect on arrival.

💬

Student Counselling

One-on-one sessions to address doubts, resolve concerns, and provide emotional and academic guidance throughout your journey.
